Common questions

What is the app?
Fifty-one guided walks across six cities: London, Rome, Stockholm, Gothenburg, Colombo and Istanbul. Each walk is a set of stops at real places you can stand in today, narrated by someone who lived there at the time.
Do I have to be in the city?
No. Every walk works from anywhere. Being there adds something, but nothing is locked behind your location.
What does it cost?
Nothing while we are building it.
Are the images real photographs?
No. Every image in the app is a reconstruction generated by an AI model, based on the historical record. None of them are archive photographs.
What is the guide, and is it AI?
Yes. The guide you can ask questions of is a generative language model, kept to the place and period of the walk you are on. It can be wrong. Treat what it says the way you would treat a knowledgeable stranger, not a source.
Why is my city not there?
We build the cities people ask for. Vote for yours in the app and it goes on the list we work from.
Why does the app ask for my location?
Only to place you on the walk and know which stop you are near. Location is coarse, not precise, and the walks work without it.
How do I delete my account and my data?
In the app, open your profile from the icon on the map and choose Delete account. It removes the account and everything attached to it, and it cannot be undone. Write to us if you would rather we did it for you.
Something in a tour is wrong.
Tell us which walk and which stop. Corrections are welcome and we would rather fix a fact than defend it.
